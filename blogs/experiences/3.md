## Role and Application

## First Round
- Gave OA in Python, solved 2 LC hard, and 1 LC medium and 1 LC medium in 28 minutes out of total of 120 minutes given, got full score.

## Onsite Rounds
- Onsite rounds were:
- System Design Android focused: Really difficult bar raiser round, question was super vague generic and modular component that is applicable to multiple teams and data sources. I communicated to get clear requirements, but didn't get much feedback. Interviewer was respectful.
- Android coding round, had to complete functions and make the app work as expected in the designs in Kotlin.
- DSA Coding, LC hard question (previous Google marked), did it in Python and went well, follow up was multi-threaded and distributed variant of the same question, never saw that before, communicated my thoughts and approach for it.
- Behavioral round, was again, really difficult as compared to any standard LPs, interviewer was friendly, and helped me ease up.

## Results
