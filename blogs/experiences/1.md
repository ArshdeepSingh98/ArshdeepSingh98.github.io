# <u> Meta Onsite Experience</u>

### **👤 By Arshdeep**  &emsp;&emsp;    🕒 January 29, 2025  
---
<br>

I cleared Meta's Onsite Loop for SWE Product E4 position but still got on hold.
  
## Key Takeaway
- It's important to double check information aside from recruiter's word. 
- In my case, they didn't considered my case as told by my recruiter based on "MQs"/ Minimum Qualifications of 2 years of experience, I had 1 year 11 months and 9 days, felt short by 21 days.

## Role and Application
- Sourcer reached out to me directly based on my LinkedIn profile.
- I then had an initial conversation with her about my background and experience and then applied with my resume.

## First Round
- I gave my first in May 2024, I was asked 3 medium level LC questions, with 2 follow ups (5 total) in 45 minutes.
- Solved all of them, got really positive feedback and move forward to next round in the next morning from the recruiter
- After a week, based on my resume, they told me I had to wait 9 months after graduation, due to new policy not allowing recent graduates to be considered for roles other than university hire roles.

## Onsite Rounds
- After around 8 months, I started my process again in December.
- 1. System Design round was from the common HLD questions adjacent to Auction Bidding, went well and got positive feedback from recruiter.
- 2. Two Coding rounds in one day. 
    - First round interviewer asked my 2 LC mediums, solved both of them within time limit along with verification and complexities. 
    - Second round, I solved the 2 questions quickly with optimized O(1) space complexity and best TC, I got another question (3rd), solved it as well.
    Got positive feedback for both the rounds from recruiter.
- 3. Behavioral round got delayed by 20 days due to Chrismas and Winter holidays, asked common LP questions, went well.

## Results
- Asked to get internal references to finish the process after 3 months of interviewing, I provided 3 references to my recruiter.
- They all got emails after my results, I had provided their emails to my recruiter 20 days prior.
- On 22nd Jan 2025, I got a call from recruiter that they are putting my packet on hold for next year, I didn't meet their MQs.

## Lessons 
- Keep the company landscape and timeline in mind, Layoffs and Hiring Freeze can impact ongoing processes as well.
